KOLHAPUR

Kolhapur is a historic city in Maharashtra, India, known as the "Varanasi of the South,"nestled by the Panchganga River and Sahyadri hills, blending rich Maratha heritage with modern development. Famous for its Mahalakshmi Temple, handcrafted Kolhapuri chappals (leather slippers), traditional jewelry like Kolhapuri Saaj, spicy Kolhapuri cuisine (especially non-veg), and as a hub for sugar, textile, and engineering industries, it offers a vibrant mix of culture, spirituality, and commerce.
